Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal information for the YouTube Uploader app and channel.

Data collected

We use Google OAuth to obtain permission to upload videos to your YouTube account. We request only the scopes necessary for uploading. We do not collect or store additional personal data beyond the OAuth tokens required to operate the uploader.

Storage

OAuth tokens used by the uploader are stored locally on the machine in the project's poc/token.json file. You are responsible for securing that file and the machine where the uploader runs.

Third-party services

The uploader uses YouTube Data API (Google). Please review Google's privacy policies for details on how Google handles OAuth data.
